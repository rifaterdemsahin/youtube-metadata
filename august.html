Month thesis
What August actually did, not what the 365-day dashboard already said.

What to do in the rest of August
Ten days left in the month. The reporting lag means the Bubble video should be re-read around 22–23 Aug.
  • 1. Treat Knowledge Engine as the packaging template. 47 views, 55% retention, 5 shares, 1 sub in 72 hours. Same length band (60–90s), same “one system, one visual” promise. Do not go back to 20-minute uploads as the default this month.
  • 2. Re-cut one catalog winner as a sibling short. “How to Actually Learn AI” still holds 18.6% at 16:59 and produced 18 of the month’s 71 minutes from 6 views. A 90-second “visual construction method” cut would borrow Search demand the short-form test does not have yet.
  • 3. Stop expecting Browse. Four Browse views. Thumbnails on the new shorts need a Suggested-language version (face + one object, almost no text) or they will keep living on Skool/GitHub forwards. See the CTR page.
  • 4. Wait 48 hours, then judge the Bubble video on hold, not views. 28 Data API views in ~40 hours is slower than Knowledge Engine’s 11/22/14 three-day curve, but Analytics has not scored retention. If it holds <40%, the doomsday-headline hook is selling a comment, not a watch.
  • 5. Keep the Claude intro as the end-screen target. 11 August views on an 11-second trailer published in June, 51% hold. It still drips. The last-3 set is two new shorts plus this evergreen bumper — that mix is working.
  • 6. Viewer comments are zero. All three latest comment threads are your own Skool pin. Ask a specific question on-screen in the last 5 seconds of the next short (“What’s the first vault you would wire — Drive, Git, or Obsidian?”) or the pin has nothing to sit on.
