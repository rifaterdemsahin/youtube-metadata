What retention measures
YouTube reports this as “average percentage viewed.” It is not unique viewers — it is watch time ÷ video length, averaged across views.

Hook (0–15s)

Most drop-off happens here. A retained video states the payoff in the first sentence and shows a result on screen before the intro bumper.

Hold (body)

Pattern interrupts every 20–40 seconds: a cut, a diagram, a checkpoint. Long-form on this channel sits around 7–12% because density falls in the middle third.

End (last 20%)

End screens only work if people are still there. The 66.6% masterclass intro and 59.4% Obsidian short prove length is the first lever.

How YouTube uses it

Suggested and Browse rank videos that keep similar viewers watching. A 2.3% CTR with 10% retention under-trains the algorithm versus a tighter cut with 20%+.

Retention playbook
Apply on the next upload. Pair with the CTR page — a high click with a 1% hold still loses the impression auction next time.
  • 1. Open on the result. First frame should match the thumbnail promise. No 20-second channel ident.
  • 2. Target 8 minutes or less for tutorials unless it is a live build. Channel data: under-8-minute videos cluster well above the 10.2% average.
  • 3. Recut the three traffic engines. LinkedIn Job Hunt, AI No-Code, and Agentic Era are 36.7% of views at ~8% hold. A tight recut would compound both CTR and suggested traffic.
  • 4. Chapter the body. Spoken checkpoints (“three things, then the demo”) reduce the mid-video cliff on 15+ minute videos.
  • 5. End screen only after a hold. End screens currently contribute 1 view. Retention in the last 20 seconds is the prerequisite — see End Video Closing under Operate.
