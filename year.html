What the year taught
Patterns from the catalog — not generic YouTube advice.
Format beats topic for hold 4.3×
Google Flow shorts hold ~60%. Talking-head / workshop holds ~13%. Claude Architect intro 66.6%. Length is the cheapest lever.
Tutorials under ~6 minutes 23.2%
10x Interview Rate (5:55, 23.2%) and MCP Explained (5:58, 18.7%) beat 15–25 min career videos sitting at 7–11% even when those get more views.
Long-form is a mid-video leak 7–11%
LinkedIn Job Hunt / No-Code / Agentic Era drive traffic and dump viewers. Recut as 6–8 min “one outcome” pieces. The 46-min workshop at 16% is the exception — live build energy, not slides.
Off-platform channel 64% ext+direct
You bring people in; YouTube does not discover you. Search and Suggested are both 4.4%. Community (Skool / GitHub / LinkedIn) already works. SEO and titles are the unlock.
Comments on frameworks discussion hubs
Master Complex Topics, Master AI, Resumes are Obsolete generate dialogue. Pinned Skool CTAs belong there — not only on the newest upload.
Flow shares, not yet subs 0 net Aug
6 shares in August vs 1/month before. Retention on Flow is elite. Conversion to subscribe is still flat. Hold the format; close the CTA harder.
Playbook from the data
  • 1. Open on the result. First frame should match the thumbnail promise. No 20-second channel ident.
  • 2. Target 8 minutes or less for tutorials unless it is a live build. Under-8-minute videos sit well above the 10.2% average hold.
  • 3. Recut the three traffic engines. LinkedIn Job Hunt, AI No-Code, and Agentic Era are 36.7% of views at ~8% hold.
  • 4. Chapter anything longer. Spoken checkpoints reduce the mid-video cliff on 15+ minute videos.
  • 5. End screen only after a hold. End screens contributed 1 view all year. Last-20-second retention is the prerequisite.
  • 6. Keep shipping Flow for reach, and put the subscribe / Skool ask on the 55%+ hold clips, not on the 8% lectures.
